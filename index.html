Welcome!

Nurzhan Technical Lyceum trains skilled workers for the oil and gas industry, machine-building and the city services of Nurzhan. Study is free, students receive a stipend, and most graduates are taken on by our partner enterprises.

News / Новости

23.10.2026Evening welding course for adults
A four-month evening course for manual arc welders starts on 23 November (Monday to Thursday, 18:00–21:00). 3,600 som; for registered unemployed free with a referral from the employment office. Certificate of the 3rd grade. Тел. 41-18-24.
01.09.20261 September
312 new students began their studies in eleven groups. The welders' group is again full; the cooks' group was opened for the first time since 2019.
27.06.2026Graduation 2026
286 graduates received diplomas. 191 have places at NurGaz, Nurzhanmash, the refinery and the Nurzhan heating enterprise. Congratulations!
14.04.2026Skills contest
Our students took 1st and 3rd places in welding and 2nd in electrical installation at the city contest of young workers held by Nurzhanmash.
02.02.2026Frost
Classes on 2–4 February are cancelled because the workshops cannot be heated below −25°. Practice at the enterprises continues.
