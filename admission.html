Admission 2027 / Приём

Documents are accepted from 1 June to 25 August 2027, Monday to Friday 9:00–16:00, room 12.

Documents

  1. Application (form at the lyceum)
  2. Certificate of basic (9 grades) or general secondary (12 grades) education, original
  3. Passport or birth certificate, copy
  4. Medical certificate form 086
  5. 6 photographs 3×4
  6. For the hostel: certificate of residence

Conditions

No entrance exams: places are given by the average mark of the certificate. Study is free. Stipend 450 som a month (650 som for good marks); students on practice at NurGaz receive the company's practice allowance. Hostel 150 som a month.

Приём документов с 1 июня по 25 августа 2027 года, кабинет 12.
