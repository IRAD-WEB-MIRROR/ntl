23.10.2026Evening welding course for adults
A four-month evening course for manual arc welders starts on 23 November (Monday to Thursday, 18:00–21:00). 3,600 som; for registered unemployed free with a referral from the employment office. Certificate of the 3rd grade. Тел. 41-18-24.
01.09.20261 September
312 new students began their studies in eleven groups. The welders' group is again full; the cooks' group was opened for the first time since 2019.
27.06.2026Graduation 2026
286 graduates received diplomas. 191 have places at NurGaz, Nurzhanmash, the refinery and the Nurzhan heating enterprise. Congratulations!
14.04.2026Skills contest
Our students took 1st and 3rd places in welding and 2nd in electrical installation at the city contest of young workers held by Nurzhanmash.
02.02.2026Frost
Classes on 2–4 February are cancelled because the workshops cannot be heated below −25°. Practice at the enterprises continues.
04.12.2025Frost: workshops closed
Practical lessons in the workshops on 4–5 December are cancelled (−27°). Theory lessons in the main building go on.
28.06.2025Graduation 2025
271 graduates. 183 were taken on by NurGaz, Nurzhanmash, the refinery and the heating enterprise.
17.03.2025Welding robot from NurGaz
NurGaz gave the lyceum a semi-automatic welding station; the welders of the 3rd year learn on it from April.
01.09.20241 September
305 new students in eleven groups. The car mechanics' group is full for the first time in five years.
21.05.2024Skills contest of Nurzhanmash
1st place in turning and 2nd in welding.
10.01.2024Smog days
On days when the city declares an air alert the hostel students do sport indoors only.
02.10.2023Hostel heating
The heating in the hostel is on from 2 October. Students who froze in the last week of September: thank you for your patience.
30.06.2022Graduation 2022
259 graduates; 168 with jobs at partner enterprises.
01.09.2020Lessons again
After the quarantine the lyceum opens for all groups. Practice at NurGaz resumes from 14 September.
20.03.2020Quarantine
Under Executive Directive 2020/05 the lyceum is closed from 19 March. Hostel students have gone home. Wait for the call of your master.
12.09.2017New lathes
Four new lathes for workshop No. 1, under the NurGaz agreement.
15.06.2014Ten years with NurGaz
Since 2004 more than 1,100 of our graduates have started work at NurGaz.
05.09.2011New director
Sergei Lim, former chief welder of Nurzhanmash and a graduate of PTU-3 (1983), is the new director of the lyceum.
24.06.2010Graduation 2010
302 graduates. Diplomas were given by the director Aleksandr Dvoretsky, who retires after 22 years.
